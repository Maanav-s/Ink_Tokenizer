Subject rules (writing and humanities):

- Mostly `text` and `list` elements: headings, bullets, numbered and nested outline lists, and
  prose. Prose can be full sentences, and a `text` element may be a whole sentence or two (it wraps
  to several written lines), but keep the page within its line budget.
- A `table` only when a problem asks to compare or sort things (for vs against, say); its cells are
  short phrases.
- No `math` elements, diagrams, circuits or truth tables.
- Use annotations (underline, circle, box, arrow) sparingly, as a student marks a thesis or links
  an idea to its rebuttal.
