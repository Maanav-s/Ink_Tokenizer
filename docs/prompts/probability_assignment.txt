ECE 313 - Problem Set 4: Probability (fake assignment for synthetic pages)

1. A fair coin is flipped 3 times. Let X be the number of heads.
   (a) List every outcome and the value of X for each.
   (b) Give the PMF of X as a table.
   (c) Compute E[X] and Var(X).

2. A sensor flags a faulty chip with probability 0.95 when the chip is faulty,
   and falsely flags a good chip with probability 0.02. 1% of chips are faulty.
   (a) Find P(flagged).
   (b) Find P(faulty | flagged) using Bayes' rule.

3. Events A and B have P(A) = 0.4, P(B) = 0.5 and P(A and B) = 0.2.
   (a) Find P(A or B).
   (b) Are A and B independent? Justify.
   (c) Fill in the 2x2 joint probability table for A, not A, B, not B.

4. A link drops each packet independently with probability 0.1. 5 packets are sent.
   (a) Find P(exactly 2 dropped).
   (b) Find P(at least 1 dropped).
